# NovaMarket product image assets

The image importer uses illustrative stock photographs hosted by Pexels and copies them into the project's own MinIO bucket. The website does not request images from Pexels at runtime.

Pexels states that its photos may be used free for commercial projects under the Pexels License (see https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042295174-What-is-the-license-of-the-photos-and-videos-on-Pexels).

## Important
These are representative category photographs, not verified official product photographs for the exact Samsung, Apple, Sony, LG, Nike, or other listed models. Do not present them as manufacturer-authenticated product shots.
